💡 How does it work?

📤 Share bandwidth → Earn credit

Share idle bandwidth to earn credit immediately. The higher your credit, the bigger your consumption discount (up to 30% off). Share during peak hours to double your credit rewards.

💰 When others consume → Earn flow currency

When others use your shared bandwidth, your flow currency arrives in real time. Use flow currency to request bandwidth and create a good incentive loop.

🤝 Team up → Save more

Form a mutual-aid group with roommates. When consuming, the system matches group members first and gives an extra 8% saving. The saved costs inside the group are distributed by contribution ratio.

⚖️ Share more to use more

The system tracks your sharing ratio over the last 30 days. If you only use without sharing, the price will rise and you may be rejected. New users have a 7-day protection period.

🔐 Guarantee users to earn commission

Users with good credit can guarantee newcomers to help them get started quickly. For every consumption made by the guaranteed user, you automatically receive a 1% commission.

🔬 Game-Theory Principles (Geek Mode)

Repeated game

Long-term accounts turn a one-shot game into an infinitely repeated game. The discount factor δ = 0.5 + 0.005 × credit + 0.1 × recent sharing rate makes cooperation a rational equilibrium strategy.

Reputation mechanism

Credit scores address information asymmetry and form a separating equilibrium: high-credit users get discounts, while low-credit users are isolated—encouraging truthful behavior.

Coalition game

Shapley values fairly distribute coalition benefits based on marginal contributions: φᵢ = Σ (|S|!(n-|S|-1)!/n!) × [v(S∪{i}) - v(S)], preventing free-riding inside coalitions.

Mechanism design

Dynamic pricing (bandwidth scarce +10% / abundant -10%) + linear rejection probability (0% contribution ratio => 100% rejection) + wash-trading detection achieve incentive compatibility.
